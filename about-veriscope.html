Function Media LLC · VERISCOPE™

Operational intelligence infrastructure for high-consequence environments.

VERISCOPE™ by Function Media LLC is a technology platform focused on organizing fragmented evidence, preserving provenance, identifying relationships, and supporting accountable human review in complex mission environments.

VERISCOPE™ sits within the Function Media LLC technology portfolio alongside SAFEPLATE™ for food-safety intelligence and NORTHLINE™ for K–12 district operations intelligence.

The VERISCOPE™ family includes VERISCOPE DEFENSE™, VERISCOPE CYBER™, and VERISCOPE JURIS™. Public descriptions focus on mission, governance, evidence, and system-level capabilities without disclosing proprietary implementation details.

Function Media LLC was founded by Illya Knight, Founder and Managing Member. Shea Johnson is Co-Owner.
